# Responsible AI

FlowFreeze is decision support, not an autonomous enforcement system. Outputs are recommendations and require an authorized human decision. The system keeps structured evidence, model confidence, taint method, collateral estimate, and audit trail. Free-form LLM output is optional, server-side, grounded in retrieved evidence, and prohibited from changing risk scores or action parameters. Human review, appeal, privacy controls, bias testing, drift monitoring, and production authorization are required before real MFS use.
